📈Forex: what is a currency pair?Trading basics · 4 min read＋

A currency pair shows the value of one currency against another, such as EUR/USD. The first currency is the base currency and the second is the quote currency.

Remember: Learn how spreads, leverage and volatility work. Practise on a demo account before considering live trading.

🧮Risk management: protect your capitalTrading basics · 5 min read＋

Risk management means deciding how much you can afford to lose before entering a trade. Define your stop loss, calculate position size and avoid increasing risk to recover a loss.

There is no guaranteed strategy or guaranteed profit in trading.

⛓️Blockchain explained simplyWeb3 · 3 min read＋

A blockchain is a shared digital record maintained by a network of computers. Transactions are grouped into blocks and linked together, making past records difficult to alter.

Always research projects carefully. A blockchain transaction may be irreversible.

🔐Keep your wallet and seed phrase safeOnline safety · 4 min read

Your seed phrase can give full control of a crypto wallet. Never share it with anyone, never type it into a link sent by a stranger, and keep a secure offline backup.

Real support staff should never ask you to reveal your seed phrase.

🛡️Steps to secure an online accountAccount recovery · 3 min read＋

Use a unique password, enable two-factor authentication, keep recovery email and phone details current, and review active sessions regularly.

If locked out, use the service's official recovery page. Never pay strangers who promise guaranteed recovery.

🚨How to recognise an online scamScam protection · 3 min read

Warning signs include urgent threats, guaranteed returns, requests for OTPs or passwords, suspicious links and pressure to send money quickly.

Pause, verify through an official channel and report suspicious accounts or messages.

📱Basic smartphone troubleshootingTech support · 3 min read

Restart your phone, check your connection, update the app, confirm available storage and review app permissions. Back up important data before major resets.

🔒Browse the internet more safelySafety tips · 3 min read

Keep your browser updated, use trusted websites, avoid unknown downloads, check website addresses carefully and do not reuse passwords.
